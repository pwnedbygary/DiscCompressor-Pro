DiscCompressor Pro 2.2.2 converts DiscJuggler (CDI) images, the usual format of Dreamcast games on CD-R, which chdman can't read. It also looks inside ISO, CSO, ZSO and DAX images to choose between CD and DVD CHDs, so PlayStation and PlayStation 2 CD games no longer become DVD CHDs. And it has a new logo.

## What's new since 2.1.2

### DiscJuggler (CDI) images

DiscCompressor Pro now reads DiscJuggler 2.0, 3.0 and 3.5 images itself:

| From   | To                        |
| ------ | ------------------------- |
| CDI    | CHD, or BIN/CUE (extract) |
| CD CHD | CDI (extract)             |

- **Dreamcast CD-Rs still boot in Flycast after converting.** These discs keep their data track in a second session, which a CHD can't record. The CHD puts the track where Flycast looks for it, the BIN/CUE marks the sessions in its cue sheet, and extracting the CHD back to CDI, the new **CDI** choice under **Extract**, gives the disc its second session back.
- **CHDs made from CDIs open in Flycast 2.7.** Flycast 2.7 and earlier can't open a CHD whose tracks have pregaps (the short gap before a track), so CHDs made from CDI images have none: the pregap before a later audio track becomes the end of the track before it, sound included, and the data track's pregap is left out, as the gap between the sessions covers it.
- **Extracting the CHD of a Dreamcast CD-R to BIN/CUE** now writes one BIN per track, with the sessions marked, the way Flycast's cue reader needs it. Other CDs still get chdman's single BIN.
- Every track stays at the position the image records. The console warns when the converted disc may not start, for example when the image has more than two sessions or its second session starts earlier than usual, and when subchannel data, which a few images store, is left out.
- The job settings show the DiscJuggler version and list the tracks of each session. **Help → Formats** explains how Dreamcast CD-Rs are converted.

Tested with the 240p Test Suite, a freely distributable Dreamcast homebrew disc, and a variant of it with two audio tracks: every CHD, BIN/CUE and CDI made from them boots in Flycast 2.7, and Flycast's own image-reading code finds every track at its original address. Converting the 240p Test Suite's CDI to CHD and back gives the same audio and data sectors; only the data track's 150-sector pregap, which the CHD doesn't keep, comes back as zeros.

Known issues:

- Flycast 2.7 and earlier play the CD audio (music tracks) of a CD-R from a CHD distorted, because they read the samples in the wrong byte order. Flycast's development builds fix this, and CDI and BIN/CUE aren't affected, so keep games with CD audio as CDI or BIN/CUE until a Flycast release includes the fix.
- A BIN/CUE of a Dreamcast CD-R whose cue sheet has pregaps (`INDEX 00` or `PREGAP` lines) still becomes a CHD with pregaps, as chdman makes it, and Flycast 2.7 refuses it with "Unsupported subtype or pre/postgap". The console warns about this and suggests the fix: extract that CHD to CDI and convert the CDI to CHD. The same fix works for such CHDs made with chdman elsewhere; recompressing a CHD keeps its pregaps. Redump's GD-ROM cue sheets aren't affected: chdman removes their pregaps itself.

### CD or DVD CHDs, chosen for you

chdman stores a disc as a CD CHD (`createcd`) or a DVD CHD (`createdvd`), and emulators expect the type that matches the original disc. BIN/CUE, GDI and CDI images always use `createcd`, but an ISO, CSO, ZSO or DAX image can come from either kind of disc, and 2.1 made DVD CHDs of all of them unless you chose CD. The new **Auto** media type, now the default, looks at the disc to decide:

- **DVD:** PSP UMDs, DVD-Video discs, and GameCube, Wii and Xbox discs.
- **CD:** PlayStation discs.
- **PlayStation 2:** DVD if the image has the UDF file system that PS2 DVDs carry, or PCSX2 would treat it as a DVD; otherwise CD.
- **Anything else:** DVD if it's larger than a CD can hold or has a UDF file system, otherwise CD. Some tools add a UDF file system to CD images too; choose **CD** for those.

The job settings give the reason, for example "This is a PlayStation 2 CD, so Auto makes a CD CHD (createcd)", and **DVD** and **CD** are still there if you want the other type. For CSO, ZSO and DAX images, the app reads the disc inside the compressed file.

## Upgrading from 2.1

- **ISOs of CD-based discs now become CD CHDs.** That includes PlayStation and PlayStation 2 CD games in ISO, CSO, ZSO or DAX form, which 2.1 turned into DVD CHDs by default, and other images that fit on a CD and show no sign of being a DVD. To keep making DVD CHDs, choose **DVD** under **Media type** in the job settings and click **Save as default**.
- **Your default media type** becomes **Auto** if it was DVD, even if you chose DVD yourself, because 2.1 saved that choice just like its own default. If you had chosen CD, it stays CD; to let the app decide instead, choose **Auto** and click **Save as default**. Queue files saved by 2.1 or 1.x load the same way.
- To turn a DVD CHD that 2.1 made of a CD game into a CD CHD, extract it to ISO and convert the ISO again. Recompressing the CHD won't help, because recompressing keeps a CHD's type.
- Coming from 1.x? Read the [2.1.2 release notes](https://github.com/pwnedbygary/DiscCompressor-Pro/releases/tag/v2.1.2) first: 2.x is a rewrite, and they explain how your settings carry over.
- Versions 2.2.0 and 2.2.1 were tagged but never released. If you tried a 2.2.0 test build, convert your CDI images again: the CHDs and BIN/CUE files it made from them, and BIN/CUE files it extracted from those CHDs, don't load in Flycast 2.7, and recompressing a CHD doesn't change that. Without the CDI, extract such a CHD to CDI with 2.2.2 and convert that CDI to CHD. Files made by a 2.2.1 test build are fine: 2.2.2 only stops a wrong warning about GD-ROM cue sheets.
